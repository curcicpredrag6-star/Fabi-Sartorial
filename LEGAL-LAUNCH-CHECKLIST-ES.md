# Sartorial — legal and operational checklist (Spain)

This is a drafting checklist, not final legal advice or publishable legal wording. Fill placeholders only after the seller is legally identified.

## Legal Notice / Aviso legal
Seller legal name; legal form; NIF/CIF; registered address; effective contact email/phone; public-register details where applicable.

## Privacy / Política de privacidad
Identify the controller; explain purposes and legal bases separately (pre-contract/contract, legal obligations, legitimate interests where appropriate, optional consent); list data categories, processors/recipients, transfers, retention criteria, rights and AEPD complaint route.

Data scope: contact and delivery details, order selections, garment measurements, correspondence and transaction references. Do not request diagnoses, disability details, medical records or body photos. Restrict access to measurements and delete/anonymise them when no longer needed, subject to legal retention obligations for transaction records.

## Cookies
Essential cookies only before consent. Provide equally accessible accept, reject and settings options. Document categories/vendors and allow consent withdrawal/change. Do not load optional analytics/marketing tags before consent where required.

## Terms and consumer information
Explain product characteristics, configuration steps, contract language, total price including taxes, shipping costs, payment, production/delivery estimates, order confirmation, error correction, complaint channel, legal guarantees and withdrawal rules.

## Personalised goods
Do not publish a blanket “no returns” statement. The withdrawal exception for goods made to consumer specifications or clearly personalised is limited; statutory remedies for defective/non-conforming goods remain. Have counsel review the exact products and order flow.

## Secure order/payment architecture
Browser submits to server; server validates options and price; creates pending order; payment session is created server-side; payment is confirmed only by verified signed webhook; confirmation is sent and order stored securely. Never expose secrets in browser JavaScript or store card data on Sartorial servers.
